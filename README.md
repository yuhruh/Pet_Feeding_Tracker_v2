Using RoR, Heroku and Chartkick to record and monitor cat feeding amount a day with dry and wet food. The app can show the raw data, column chart and favorite score, and also see the feeding trends according to the chart and discover what your cat loves.

https://feeding-tracker-v2-3e86df55d613.herokuapp.com/

### Requirements
- Sign In/Sign Out/Sign Up, having basic validation on email and password/ timezone automatic select when sign up.

- add/edit/delete records.

- edit/delete users by their own.

- show top 5 highest score of favorites food.

- automatically show the current date and time when inputing a new record, date can not be a future date and time can not be records in (current time + 5 minute).

- show the column chart following the records.

- pagination for pets and records.

- add two new columns for weight to help owner track body condition and note for more information to add it.
